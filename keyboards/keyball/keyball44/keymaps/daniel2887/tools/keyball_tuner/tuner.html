Keyball Tuner

Welcome to the advanced acceleration tuner for Keyball keyboards.
Connect your device to visualize your sensor data and tune your acceleration curve.

Getting Started

  • Ensure your Keyball is connected via USB.
  • Click the Connect button in the top right.
  • Select your device from the popup list (Usage Page 0xFF60).
How to use:
1. Connect to your keyboard (Usage Page 0xFF60).
2. Global Sensitivity sets the base speed multiplier.
3. Adjust Curve to change acceleration relative to input speed.
* Y-Axis Max is set manually below. Points are clamped to this max.
* Curve is strictly monotonic (dragging one point pushes others).
